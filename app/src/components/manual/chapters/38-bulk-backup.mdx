From the home screen, two icons in the bottom-right corner handle whole-library transfer.

- **Export.** Writes a single ZIP of every map (named with today's date). This is the recommended backup.
- **Import.** Restores from such a ZIP, or imports a single JSON, with the same preview and duplicate-detection flow described in <ChapterLink id="importing-existing" />.

<Note>

Make a backup before risky operations (bulk deletes, large imports, moving your data folder). Restoring is just a re-import of the ZIP.

</Note>

To backup your data otherwise:
1. You can use **Git** on your app data folder (see <ChapterLink id="first-run" />).
2. You can point your data folder at a network location (**Settings → Application → Data → Change folder**).

Individual maps can also be mirrored to map-making.app or GeoGuessr with the sync plugins, though those are meant for keeping a remote map current rather than as a backup. See <ChapterLink id="builtin-plugins" />.
