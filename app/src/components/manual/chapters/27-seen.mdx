When enabled, <App /> records every panorama you actually look at, across all maps, into a "seen" history. It is useful for going back to locations you have previously viewed.

## Recording

Recording is controlled in **Settings → Editing → Seen**. Turn **Log viewed panos** on or off, optionally **Save thumbnails**, and choose the **thumbnail resolution** (Low 160x90, Medium 320x180, High 640x360) to trade storage for clarity.

## Browsing

The **Seen** button in the bottom bar opens the history with thumbnails, a flag, and the address or coordinates of each visit. Filter by:

- **Country.** A dropdown of countries seen.
- **Map.** A dropdown of maps.
- **Address search.** A text box that matches the stored address.

The history is paginated and capped at the most recent 10,000 entries, pruning the oldest as new ones arrive.

## On the map

**Toggle seen locations overlay** (command palette) draws every seen pano as a dot on the current map. Dots are colored by whether the pano is already on this map: blue means it is, and clicking opens that location; orange means it is only in history, and clicking previews the pano read-only with the option to add it. Nothing is added to the map until you say so.

This is the fast way to see where you have already been while filling in a region - the orange dots are the panos you looked at and did not keep.
