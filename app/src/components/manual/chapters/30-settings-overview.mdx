export const title = "App settings vs per-map settings";

<App /> has two distinct layers of settings. Knowing which is which avoids confusion.

<Img name="settings.png" caption="App settings, split into Street View, Map, Editing, Keyboard, Application, Integrations, and Advanced sections." />

## App settings (global)

Opened from the gear icon (bottom-right). These apply everywhere and are stored on your machine, not on the map. Sections are listed down the left; the search box above them filters every section at once, including shortcuts, which is usually faster than hunting through them.

- **Street View.** Navigation and movement defaults, which overlay controls are visible, what the fullscreen panorama shows, and the date picker.
- **Map.** Map pan speed and the Alt slow-down; the default, active, and staged marker colors; panorama dot color and sizing (constant on screen vs growing as you zoom in); default polygon color; the country/subdivision border datasets; and what the fullscreen map shows. Marker _size_ is per-map, not here.
- **Editing.** Tag display and suggestions, the seen history, and your geocoding provider.
- **Keyboard.** Every rebindable shortcut. See <ChapterLink id="controls-hotkeys" />.
- **Application.** Session restore, which fields show on map rows, updates, and where your data folder lives.
- **Integrations.** Discord Rich Presence and the local REST API.
- **Advanced.** Custom CSS and debug tools.

Fullscreen has two independent modes with their own settings: the fullscreen panorama (under **Street View**) and the fullscreen map (under **Map**).

## Per-map settings

Opened from the **Map settings** dropdown above the map. These belong to the current map only:

- **Selecting new locations.** Point view along the road and a **Direction** preference (forwards, backwards, most northern/eastern/southern/western, or random); prefer official over unofficial; prefer higher quality over newer images; disallow unofficial; use Pano ID locations by default; and a **Min search radius** slider.
- **Map behaviour.** Show location previews on hover, and **Select-only mode** (map clicks never add locations).
- **Scoring.** The score bounds editor.
- **Display.** Marker style (Pin, Circle, Camera direction arrow), marker size, **Display 5K radius**, and **Show click search radius at cursor**.

Enrichment is also per-map, but it lives in its own dialog rather than this dropdown. See <ChapterLink id="enrichment" />.
